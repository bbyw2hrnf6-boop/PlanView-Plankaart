## Lossless context packaging

The browser loads `data/netherlands_context.json.gz`, `data/camping_sites.geojson.gz` and `data/development_projects.geojson.gz` with the shared gzip-aware loader. Readable maintenance copies remain local; publication omits only those duplicate plaintext files. Decompression reproduces every source byte, including all geometry, metadata and features. `update_context.py` and `update_development_projects.py` regenerate both copies; release validation rejects any mismatch. This reduces transfer and hosting-archive size without lowering map or search quality.

## Parcel and surroundings search — October 2026

The search now combines 17 selectable zoning groups (with exclusions), plan status, point-level bouwvlak/building checks, and **whole-parcel criteria**: registered parcel area min/max, minimum mapped bouwvlak overlap in m² or percent, maximum BAG building coverage in percent, exclusion of mapped Natura 2000 (Netherlands) and minimum parcel-edge distance to those areas. Set BAG coverage to 0% to screen the entire resolved parcel for mapped buildings; an empty seed point alone is not an empty parcel. Potential-building-land requests enable this whole-parcel check. Different chosen criteria combine with AND. Accepted alternatives within zoning groups, plan statuses and soil classes combine with OR; cross-field alternatives such as “near a station OR near a park” remain unsupported and need separate searches.

The manual form exposes independent min/max distances for **12 surroundings types**: campsite/holiday park, town, railway station, park, sports grounds, cemetery, swimming pool, marina, industrial land, hospital, allotment gardens and recreation area. Geography is mapped European Netherlands only; foreign amenities and foreign Natura 2000 areas are outside these datasets. Full TOP10NL polygon boundaries, including holes and multipolygons, replace bounding extents for geometric distance screening. Source imagery/data dates remain distinct from the download date. Compressed per-category geometry files load only when requested. The refreshed camping category includes `vakantiepark`.

`parcel-screen.js` resolves a single BRK parcel at the point, prefers its registered area, clips/merges BAG footprints and plan-specific bouwvlakken against the entire parcel, and measures Natura distances from the parcel boundary. Numeric overlap areas and distances are approximate map measurements. Incomplete data may establish a decisive lower bound, but never absence. Errors, ambiguous parcel boundaries and unfulfilled data completeness remain visible as open checks. Results are deduplicated by parcel plus source plan. Sources, timestamps and parcel evidence are retained in comparison snapshots and JSON exports.

The scan still samples locations and caps returned candidates; it is **not a complete inventory of every parcel**, a land-for-sale service or a proof of buildability. The selected Wro plan may be superseded; current DSO rule review remains necessary. Natura criteria concern mapped protected areas in the Netherlands and do not decide permit obligations. Qualitative quietness, flood safety, ownership, price and engineering suitability remain unverified. Numeric modeled Lden, AHN slope, BRO soil classes and GHG depth are executable screening criteria with the source/scope limits below.

Rebuild the source snapshots with `python3 update_context.py` and `python3 update_bouwvlakken.py`. The latter also rebuilds `data/bouwvlakken/spatial-tiles.json` using full geometry extents, so shapes crossing the representative-point tile boundary are included in parcel overlap checks. For an existing unchanged extract, `python3 update_envelope_spatial_index.py` rebuilds that spatial index alone. Never combine its metadata with another extract revision. [Data sources and next integrations](docs/PDOK_SEARCH_SOURCES_2026-10-17.md) records the research, including RIVM modeled noise, AHN slope, flood scenarios and BRO soil limitations.

Example: “Grundstücke zwischen 500 und 2000 m², mindestens 100 m² Baufenster, maximal 10 % bebaut, mindestens 500 m Abstand zu Natura 2000 und höchstens 3 km zum Krankenhaus.” Review interpreted thresholds before running.


A static, local-first research prototype for planning maps across all 12 Netherlands provinces. The street basemap stays visible at every zoom; the map button cycles Street, BGT detail and Aerial. The default clean rendering keeps plan fills translucent so roads and building outlines remain legible. On macOS, double-click `PlanView starten.command` on the Desktop. ## Data method and limits

A click sends PDOK `GetFeatureInfo` requests for the exact point. The returned `enkelbestemming` geometry supplies the main designation and IMRO plan ID. The app matches that ID to the `plangebied` document metadata and ranks the direct geometry-linked plan above unrelated municipality documents. Parcel geometry comes from the cadastral WMS. Other enabled planning overlays are fetched after the primary result. Check current legal effect and supplementary rules in the Omgevingsloket.

The **Advanced search** lives under the top search bar. Choose the current map view, one municipality, a selected province, all Netherlands, or a drawn rectangle. Choosing municipality scope while the sidebar still shows all municipalities uses a municipality named in the question, or the municipality under the map center when zoomed in. A request for the “marked area” starts rectangle drawing if none exists, then resumes the search. The search uses spatial tiles derived from the [official PDOK Bouwvlak download](https://www.pdok.nl/atom-downloadservices/-/article/ruimtelijke-plannen). Small scopes load full local index tiles; large scopes load a 0.01-degree spatial overview to keep the browser responsive. Candidate points are inside actual Netherlands bouwvlak polygons. A candidate qualifies only when the zoning feature at that point references the same IMRO plan and designation polygon as the indexed bouwvlak; results show the precise bouwvlak outline, ID, plan status and date. PDOK `GetFeatureInfo` remains a fallback for other sampled points. Sites missing a confirmed envelope at the tested point appear only as separately labeled review leads. The index covers the European Netherlands, while **search results still screen a bounded sample of candidate points, not every parcel**. This avoids claiming complete site discovery.

The search also reads a compact, locally cached [TOP10NL OGC Features](https://api.pdok.nl/kadaster/brt-top10nl/ogc/v1/collections?f=json) snapshot of campsites, populated places, industrial areas, parks, sports grounds, cemeteries, swimming-pool complexes, and marinas for the European Netherlands, plus [ProRail Spoorwegen](https://www.pdok.nl/ogc-webservices/-/article/spoorwegen) station points. Regenerate it with `python3 update_context.py`; the resulting `data/netherlands_context.json` is committed for fast, dependable local use and GitHub Pages compatibility. The user question and a bounded map/context summary go to GPT-6 Luna for validated filter interpretation. If Codex or an API key is unavailable, the UI clearly labels its limited local parser. Candidate points are screened by distances to retained feature geometry; extent-only fallback evidence is explicitly uncertain. PDOK `GetFeatureInfo` checks zoning polygons, IMRO sources and mapped building footprints at those points. Matching **plan polygons**, exact bouwvlak outlines and numbered points appear on the map with distance evidence. For potential-building-land searches, areas that fit the surroundings but fail a bouwvlak or unbuilt check appear as dashed orange **review leads**, separately from candidates that meet the checked map criteria. Qualitative quietness remains an open preference; an explicit maximum modeled Lden is checked against the connected RIVM raster. TOP10NL, ProRail, and plan data have different update schedules; mapped distances remain approximate even with full polygon geometry. The supported nearby-object types live in `search_filters.schema.json`, `smart-search.js`, and `update_context.py`; add a source and mapping in all three when extending the vocabulary. An unbuilt bouwvlak does not prove ownership, availability or a building right. A complete inventory would additionally need paginated spatial indexing of all parcels and current legal-effect checks; the current bounded sample must not be represented as that inventory.

For the local Codex route, check `codex login status`. If it is not signed in, run `codex login` once in Terminal and complete the normal ChatGPT sign-in, then restart PlanView. Each advanced-search submission invokes a short-lived, read-only `codex exec` process with `gpt-6-luna`, `model_reasoning_effort=high`, and a JSON output schema. The model translates words into filters; PDOK supplies the actual plan evidence. The server binds only to `127.0.0.1`, validates the browser origin and request size, and never serves dotfiles or Python source. Search text goes through your Codex account to OpenAI; the UI shows which interpreter is active. If neither Codex nor an API key is available, the limited local phrase parser remains usable. No credentials belong in this repository or in GitHub Pages.

The optional [**NOVEX planned housing locations** WMS](https://www.pdok.nl/-/regionale-woondeals-en-novex-woningbouwlocaties-beschikbaar-op-pdok) draws officially published housing locations across its available coverage. It does not cover all potential sites in Limburg. Advanced search can require a building envelope and no mapped building at sampled points. This is a research clue, not a probability or permission to build. A residential designation does not prove that land is vacant, available, buildable, or permissible for a proposal. Assessment needs current Omgevingsplan rules, parcels, environmental constraints and authority review. PDOK also publishes [planned land use from DSO](https://www.pdok.nl/ogc-webservices/-/article/gepland-landgebruik-dso-lv-omgevingswet-inspire-geharmoniseerd), but its designations likewise do not certify buildability. The Wro spatial plans service used by this prototype is [updated semiannually](https://www.pdok.nl/ogc-webservices/-/article/ruimtelijke-plannen).

## Source-backed development and campsite context

The **Development Context** section keeps the existing NOVEX WMS unchanged and includes **Provincial Planning** and **Planned Housing & Construction Projects**. Provincial Planning reads a local GeoJSON snapshot of [Provincie Limburg planning geometries](https://portal.prvlimburg.nl/geodata/PROVINCIALE_BELEIDSPLANNEN/wfs): urban areas, nature network and groundwater protection. It is strategic context, not a parcel-level building right; current legal effect must be checked in the [Omgevingsloket](https://omgevingswet.overheid.nl/regels-op-de-kaart/). At province zoom, only urban polygons render; finer protection geometries appear as the user zooms in. The projects layer reads `data/development_projects.geojson`; its initial verified entry is Maastricht's [KPN-Vikingsite](https://www.gemeentemaastricht.nl/stad/actueel/persbericht/voormalige-kpn-locatie-ontwikkelt-zich-tot-levendige-plek-binnen-het) announcement. Its marker location is approximate, not a project boundary, and other municipalities have no imported project records yet. All overlays filter to the visible area and load only when switched on.

**Camping & Holiday Parks** appears under **Context**. `data/camping_sites.geojson` contains 252 campsite, caravan-park and bungalow-park polygons from [PDOK TOP10NL functional-area features](https://api.pdok.nl/kadaster/brt-top10nl/ogc/v1/collections/functioneel_gebied_vlak/items). `update_camping_sites.py` rebuilds the local snapshot; `update_provincial_context.py` rebuilds provincial context. Both snapshots work on localhost and GitHub Pages without a runtime data key. The campsite boundary is topographic, not a legal designation. Advanced Search distinguishes **inside** (point-in-polygon against the actual site geometry) from **near** (approximate distance to mapped boundary). Search is still sampled and not a full parcel inventory. Queries about tiny houses, chalets or permanent occupation surface the intersecting IMRO plan and a link to its official rules, while explicitly marking permission **unverified**. Current Omgevingsplan rules, use restrictions, building envelopes and occupancy terms require human review; recreation zoning alone is insufficient evidence.

## Building Insights on the local server

Click a registered building footprint to add **Building Insights** to the existing Summary panel. The browser asks the [PDOK BAG OGC API](https://www.pdok.nl/ogc-apis/-/article/basisregistratie-adressen-en-gebouwen-ba-1) for the **pand** polygon containing that exact point, then reads its BAG ID, construction year, status, use and linked **verblijfsobject** (VBO) records. Multi-unit buildings have an address selector. A click outside a registered footprint adds no building card. This BAG-only feature needs no API key, adds no map layer or colour and also works on GitHub Pages when the official BAG service is reachable.

The first **Summary** section also shows the official **Bouwvlak** at every selected plan point. Normal map clicks reuse the PDOK WMS building-envelope query when that layer is on and make a point-only query when it is off. Advanced Search selections carry the matching ID, IMRO plan, date and status from the locally indexed [PDOK Wro Bouwvlak extract](https://www.pdok.nl/atom-downloadservices/-/article/ruimtelijke-plannen) into the same panel. Each matched feature links to its source plan in Omgevingsloket and to the PDOK geometry source. The panel distinguishes a confirmed footprint, an indexed feature missing from the current point response, no footprint returned and an unavailable source. A mapped Bouwvlak is evidence of published plan geometry, not proof of a current building right.

The **gear in the top bar** opens local data settings. Paste the **DSO production** key and, separately, the **Ruimtelijke plannen V4** key. The page sends each key once to the loopback Python server. The server saves them in `~/Library/Application Support/PlanView/credentials.json` with user-only permissions; it never returns the key to the browser. Do not commit or copy that file. Empty fields keep existing keys; the Remove buttons delete them. Restart PlanView with the Desktop launcher after updating the local server files.

PDOK WMS zoning, bouwvlakken, parcels, background maps and the existing IMRO source ranking remain the base workflow and need no DSO key. The optional **DSO current plan areas** toggle adds selected-location Omgevingswet geometries, when DSO supplies a geometry ID, as purple outlines. With a configured local key, a point query to Omgevingsdocumenten Presenteren v8 supplies current documents and location IDs even when the outline layer is off; Geometrie opvragen v1 retrieves individual RD geometries; the details panel displays linked documents and source IDs. Municipality documents are put first as research candidates, not asserted as exclusive legal sources. The optional RP V4 key enriches the already-selected IMRO plan's metadata; it does not replace the PDOK plankaart. If the DSO service does not return a direct geometry/document relationship, the UI says so rather than assigning one. DSO data is loaded for a clicked location, not as a complete province-wide colored thematic tileset; the PDOK Omgevingswet vector tiles contain geometry IDs but no legal meaning on their own. Full semantic province-wide DSO rendering requires matching those IDs to DSO annotations and legal text before styling. Always use the official Omgevingsloket link for legal effect.

The static GitHub Pages build still shows PDOK and public context layers. Local keys and DSO/RP calls require `server.py` on the same desktop and are deliberately unavailable on GitHub Pages. The server proxies only fixed official DSO/RP hosts, checks loopback origin and request size, and returns document/geometry data but never key values.

## Search audit and point-specific rule text

See `docs/SEARCH_AUDIT_2026-10-17.md` for the data/API inventory, resolved defects and remaining scope gaps. Search preserves validated AI min/max/range distances, handles metres, checks explicit place names against the search scope, uses polygon geometry when available and marks extent-only distances uncertain. Distinct bouwvlakken are no longer merged by designation ID. Nearby BAG dwellings use polygon distance and reorder the final result groups.

Select a point, open Rules and ask about building, pools, use, building height or interior height. The server discovers current DSO documents and joins real STOP text to point-filtered annotations. ### Environmental screening

The local and browser search accept `environmentCriteria`: maximum modeled RIVM combined noise (`maxNoiseDb`, Lden; catalogue product name Lg), maximum parcel terrain slope (`maxSlopeDeg`), selected BRO soil main classes (`soilClasses`), and minimum modeled GHG groundwater depth in centimetres below local ground (`minGroundwaterDepthCm`). The manual form and interpreter share the same criteria. Supported numeric limits are preserved; unsupported acoustic metrics, opposite operators, soil exclusions and legal conclusions remain explicit review conditions.

AHN slope uses native 0.5 m terrain cells and requires complete coverage of the resolved parcel, including valid 3×3 neighbors. Large areas and NoData stay unknown rather than being silently resampled. Noise, soil and groundwater are point-model lookups, not measurements of the entire parcel. The regional soil map (1:50,000) cannot establish parcel suitability, bearing capacity or freedom from contamination. Groundwater is a 50 m model and missing pixels do not prove dry conditions. Lg is the RIVM catalogue product name; the original raster readme explicitly defines its numerical values as Lden. No conversion or facade-specific compliance assessment is performed.

Proven failures are excluded; missing, incomplete or unavailable evidence remains a review lead. Environmental sources, periods, units and retrieval times follow results into details, comparison and saved cases. Original search distances retain their measurement metadata when saved or compared.
